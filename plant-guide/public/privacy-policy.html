Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Brock's Plant Guide ("Plant Guide Notifications"), a private household app used to coordinate houseplant watering while the app owner is traveling.

What information we collect

The app stores plant care information, watering history, and, for the purpose of sending reminder notifications, a recipient's email address and/or mobile phone number. This contact information is entered directly by the app owner (Brock Gonzales) for a single known household member — it is not collected through any public sign-up form.

How we use this information

Contact information is used solely to send daily plant watering reminder emails and/or text messages while a trip is active. No information collected by this app is used for any other purpose.

Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Messages are sent using SendGrid (email) and Twilio (SMS) solely as delivery infrastructure, under Plant Guide Notifications' account, for the purpose described above.

Opting out

A text message recipient may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving SMS reminders, or contact the app owner directly to be removed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to brock.gonzales@gmail.com.
